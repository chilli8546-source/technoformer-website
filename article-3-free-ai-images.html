Free AI images — kyun matter karte hain?

YouTube thumbnails, Instagram posts, blog covers, presentations — har jagah high-quality images chahiye. Stock photo sites ya to paise leti hain, ya unki images har jagah dikhti hain. AI image generators is problem ka solution hain: jo socho, wahi banao — free me, 4K quality me.

Top free tools (honest comparison)

  • Text-to-image platforms: Prompt likho, image pao. Zyadatar daily free credits dete hain — roz kuch images bilkul free.
  • Image-to-image tools: Apni photo upload karo, AI use alag style me badal deta hai. Thumbnails ke liye best.
  • Reference-based generators: Ek reference image do, AI usi style/character me nayi images banata hai. Series content ke liye perfect.
Honest baat: "Unlimited free" ka दावा karne wale zyadatar tools me hidden daily caps hoti hain. Hamesha terms check karo.

Kaunsa tool kab use karein?

  • YouTube thumbnails: High-contrast, bold compositions — aise tools jo text-friendly layouts dete hain.
  • Instagram posts: Aesthetic, detailed images — quality-first generators.
  • Bulk content: Jahan roz kayi images chahiye — wahan zyada daily credits wala tool chuno.
  • Realistic photos: Photorealistic models — jo skin texture aur lighting natural rakhein.

Hidden limits se kaise bachein

  • Daily quota track karo — ek hi tool par sab kuch mat banao; 2-3 tools rotate karo.
  • Resolution check karo — kuch tools preview me achhe lagte hain par download par compress kar dete hain.
  • Watermark policy padho — free tier me kuch tools watermark lagate hain; commercial use se pehle confirm karo.
  • Prompts save karo — achhe results wale prompts notepad me rakho, dobara time bachega.
