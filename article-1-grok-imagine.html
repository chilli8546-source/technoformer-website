Grok Imagine Se Free AI Videos: Poora Guide 2026

Grok Imagine kya hai?

Grok Imagine xAI ka AI video generation feature hai. Simple words me — aap text likhte ho, aur AI usse 15-second tak ka video bana deta hai. Aur sabse badi baat? Filhal ise free me use kiya ja sakta hai, woh bhi 1080p quality me.

Paid video tools (Runway, Pika Pro jaise) mahine ke hazaron rupaye lete hain. Grok Imagine wahi kaam — cinematic clips, animations, concept videos — bina paise diye kar deta hai. Isiliye ye creators ke beech itna popular ho raha hai.

Free me video kaise banayein — steps

  1. Grok app ya website kholo — Imagine section me jao.
  2. Apna prompt likho — jitna detailed, utna behtar. Example: "Cinematic samurai fight at sunset, cherry blossoms falling, dramatic lighting, ultra realistic".
  3. Generate dabao — kuch seconds me aapka 15-second ka video ready.
  4. Download ya share karo — video ko save karke Reels/Shorts me use kar sakte ho.
Tip: Pehle ek image generate karke, phir usi image se video banana — zyada consistent results milte hain.

Limits aur quality settings

Free hone ke bawajood kuch cheezein dhyan rakhni chahiye:

  • Duration: videos 15 second tak ke hote hain — short-form content ke liye perfect.
  • Quality: 1080p output milta hai, jo YouTube Shorts aur Instagram Reels ke liye kaafi hai.
  • Daily usage: free tier me din bhar me kitne videos bana sakte ho, ye account aur region par depend karta hai. Heavy use par thoda wait karna pad sakta hai.
  • Content policy: violent ya misleading content banane ki koshish mat karo — account restrict ho sakta hai.

Pro tips

  • Prompts English me likho — AI English prompts ko behtar samajhta hai.
  • Style words add karo — "cinematic", "ultra realistic", "dramatic lighting" jaise words se quality jump karti hai.
  • Ek hi style repeat karo — agar series bana rahe ho to har prompt me same style words rakho, taaki videos consistent lagein.
  • Voiceover alag se add karo — AI video me awaaz nahi hoti; editing me apna narration daalo.

Video tutorial dekhna ho to hamara Grok Imagine full video YouTube par available hai — screen par sab kuch dikhaya gaya hai.
