AI Se Best Results: Prompt Writing Ke 7 Golden Rules

By TechnoFormer • Oct 15, 2026 • 5 min read • Category: Guide

Free AI tool ho ya paid — output ki quality 80% prompt par depend karti hai. Wahi tool, wahi free credits — bas prompt badalne se result day-night ka farq aa jata hai. Ye 7 rules har AI tool par kaam karte hain: image, video, ya text.

1. Specific raho, vague nahi

❌ "Ek achhi image banao"
✅ "Sunset par samurai warrior, cherry blossoms, cinematic lighting, ultra detailed"

AI mind reader nahi hai. Jo chahiye, wahi likho.

2. Style words use karo

Cinematic, ultra realistic, dramatic lighting, 4K, detailed — ye words AI ko quality ka signal dete hain. Har prompt me 2-3 style words rakho.

3. Negative prompts mat bhoolo

Kya nahi chahiye, ye bhi batao: "no blur, no watermark, no distorted faces". Kai tools negative prompt ka alag box dete hain.

4. Ek prompt, ek kaam

Ek hi prompt me image + video + text mat maango. Ek clear task = behtar result. Complex kaam ko steps me todo.

5. Examples do (few-shot)

Text AI ke liye: pehle ek example output dikhao, phir apna input do. AI pattern pakad leta hai aur zyada accurate jawab deta hai.

6. Iterate karo — pehla result final nahi

Pehla output pasand nahi aaya? Prompt tweak karo — ek word badlo, phir generate karo. 3-4 iterations me aam taur par best result mil jata hai.

7. Prompts save karo

Jo prompts achhe results dein, unhe notepad me save karo. Agli baar wahi structure reuse karo — sirf subject badlo. Ye pro creators ka sabse bada secret hai.

Yaad rakho: Free credits limited hote hain. Achha prompt = kam retries = zyada free usage.
