October 2026 Ke 5 Naye AI Tools Jo Aapko Pata Hone Chahiye

By TechnoFormer • Oct 19, 2026 • 5 min read • Category: Tech News

In this guide

  1. IrisGo — aapka kaam dekh kar automate karne wala AI
  2. Suno Speech — awaaz + background music ek saath
  3. ElevenLabs Eleven v4 — emotions wali voice
  4. Claude Haiku 5.5 — sasta aur tez AI model
  5. OpenAI Decisions API — developers ke liye
  6. Kaunsa try karein?

IrisGo — aapka kaam dekh kar automate karne wala AI

IrisGo ek AI operating system hai jo aapke kaam karne ka tareeka dekh kar roz ke repeating tasks (inbox, to-dos, meeting notes) khud automate kar deta hai. Beta me filhal free hai — freelancers aur solo founders ke liye dekhne layak.

Suno Speech — awaaz + background music ek saath

Suno Speech me script likho, aur ye spoken voice ke saath original background music — dono ko ek hi track me generate kar deta hai. Free plan available hai — narrated intros, bedtime stories aur motivational audio ke liye perfect.

Tip: Reels aur Shorts ke liye narration + music ka combo Suno Speech me banao, phir CapCut me video ke saath sync karo — pura workflow 10 minute ka.

ElevenLabs Eleven v4 — emotions wali voice

AI voiceovers ki sabse badi problem thi — robotic, flat awaaz. ElevenLabs ka Eleven v4 isse aage badh gaya hai: ab aap sentence ke beech me hi direction de sakte ho — "yahan thoda excited bolo, yahan dheere" — aur voice me 90+ languages me emotion aata hai. YouTube voiceovers, dubbing aur podcasts ke liye ye abhi ka best option hai. Paid plans lagbhag $6/month se start hote hain, par quality dekh kar lagta hai paisa vasool hai.

Claude Haiku 5.5 — sasta aur tez AI model

Ye developers ke liye khushkhabri hai. Claude Haiku 5.5 Anthropic ka sabse tez aur sasta chhota model hai — 1 million token context ke saath, aur keemat sirf $0.10 per 1M input tokens. Matlab classification, data extraction, customer support jaise high-volume kaam ab bahut saste me automate ho sakte hain. Agar aap coding ya automation me ho, to is model par nazar rakho.

OpenAI Decisions API — developers ke liye

OpenAI ka Decisions API text aur images ko seedha decision me badal deta hai — probability, choice ya score. Lambi explanation ke bajaye typed verdict milta hai. Content moderation, app routing aur AI agents ke decision logic banane walon ke liye bana hai. Public beta me hai, keemat $0.10 per 1M input tokens — output par koi charge nahi.

Kaunsa try karein?

  • Creator ho? → Suno Speech (free plan) aur ElevenLabs v4 try karo — audio content ka level badal jayega.
  • Freelancer/solo founder ho? → IrisGo ka beta pakdo, admin kaam automate karo.
  • Developer ho? → Claude Haiku 5.5 aur Decisions API dono dekho — saste aur powerful.

AI tools har hafte launch hote hain, par har tool aapke kaam ka nahi hota. Rule simple hai: jo tool aapka roz ka ek repeat kaam khatm kare, wahi try karo — baaki sab distraction hai. Aise hi naye tools ke honest breakdown ke liye hamara blog padhte raho.
